Personal Health Sync

Personal Health Sync is a private, single-user tool for collecting and analyzing personal health data.

What it does

The application reads authorized data from the Google Health API, including:

  • Body weight and measurements
  • Sleep duration and sleep metrics
  • Heart rate and related health metrics
  • Steps and physical activity

Information is synchronized to private Google Sheets using Google Apps Script for personal tracking, historical analysis, and progress monitoring.

Access

This application is intended for personal use by its owner. It is not offered as a public service.
