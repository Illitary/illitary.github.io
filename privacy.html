Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Overview

Personal Health Sync is a private application operated for the personal use of its owner. It connects to Google Health using OAuth 2.0.

2. Data Accessed

With explicit authorization, the application accesses the following Google Health data:

  • Weight and body measurements
  • Sleep data
  • Heart rate and related health metrics
  • Physical activity, steps, and energy expenditure

The application requests read-only access. It does not modify Google Health records.

3. Purpose of Processing

Data is used exclusively for personal health tracking, historical comparisons, and analysis.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Retrieved information is stored in private Google Sheets controlled by the application owner. Google Apps Script is used for synchronization.

At the owner's request, information stored in Google Sheets may also be accessed through ChatGPT for personal analysis and summaries.

Health data is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally made publicly accessible.

5. Authentication and Security

Google OAuth 2.0 is used for authorization. Authentication credentials are maintained within the private application environment.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Historical data is retained in the owner's Google Sheets until the owner deletes it.

Google Health authorization can be revoked through Google Account security settings. Revoking authorization prevents future access but does not automatically delete previously synchronized spreadsheet records.

7. Website

These informational pages do not contain advertising, analytics scripts, or user registration forms. Technical server access logs may be generated.

8. Contact

Privacy questions can be directed to the application support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
